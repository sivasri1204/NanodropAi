# Healthcare, Wellbeing, and UN SDG Alignment

- **SDG 3 — Good Health and Well-being:** aims to support everyday communication and awareness.
- **SDG 9 — Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure:** explores a modular, sensor-based assistive technology prototype.
- **SDG 10 — Reduced Inequalities:** focuses on accessibility and potentially lower-cost design.
- **SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities and Communities:** explores additional awareness cues that may support participation in everyday environments.

These are intended contributions, not proven outcomes. Impact should be evaluated with users and measurable tests.
